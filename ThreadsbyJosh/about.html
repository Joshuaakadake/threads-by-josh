Our Thrift Story

From Nairobi to your wardrobe – curated sustainable fashion

Born from a Simple Idea

Threads by Josh was born from that simple idea: that great style doesn't have to be new or expensive—it just has to be found.

I'm Josh, your personal curator, sifting through piles so you don't have to, finding the gems with character, quality, and a story. Every piece in the shop is something I'd personally wear or recommend.

What started as a passion for finding unique pieces has grown into a mission to make sustainable shopping accessible across Kenya. We believe in fashion that tells a story, reduces waste, and doesn't break the bank.

It's not just thrift; it's thoughtfully revived style.

Our Mission

"Our mission is simple: to make sustainable shopping effortless and exciting. We carefully select quality threads, so you can build a conscious wardrobe that looks good and does good."

What Makes Us Different

Curated Selection

Every item is hand-picked for quality, style, and durability. No low-quality "throwaways" – only pieces worth loving again.

Balanced Collection

Trendy finds meet timeless staples. Pieces that fit real lives – from office shirts to weekend jackets.

Ready to Wear

Every item is professionally cleaned, steamed, or sanitized before it reaches you. No hidden surprises.

Kenya-Wide Service

Reliable delivery across Kenya with transparent pricing. Free delivery on orders over KSh 5,000.

Our Process

1

Careful Sourcing

We source from selective suppliers and curated imports, focusing on quality, style, and durability.

2

Quality Inspection

Each piece is inspected for condition, graded honestly, and flaws are clearly documented.

3

Professional Cleaning

Items are professionally cleaned, steamed, and sanitized to ensure they're ready to wear.

4

Detailed Documentation

We provide exact measurements and detailed condition descriptions for every item.
